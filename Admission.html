INFORMATION HUB
Admissions FAQs

What is the minimum age requirement for Nursery entry?

Prospective Nursery pupils must have reached the age of 3 years by September of the entry year to ensure neurodevelopmental readiness for peer cognitive play.

Do you accept transfer students mid-session?

Yes, subject to academic vacancy in the target grade and an evaluation of the previous term's reports and transfer records.

Is there an entrance exam fee?

A nominal screening administrative fee of ₦5,000 applies to candidates for Primary & Secondary pathways.

How many terms make up an academic calendar year?

The academic session is structured around three full terms: Christmas Term (Sept - Dec), Easter Term (Jan - Apr), and Summer Term (Apr - July).

Are flexible payment options available for tuition?

While full payment is encouraged before resumption, we offer a dedicated split installment model (60% before resumption, 40% before mid-term assessments) under written agreement.
